About NeuroGen

Built by lived experience.
Powered by community.

NeuroGen was founded because a critical gap existed in Canada's support ecosystem. Neurodivergent individuals, especially those from underserved communities, had nowhere to turn that truly understood their experience.

  • Community First

    Programs designed around peer connection, not clinical distance.

  • Equity Centered

    Cultural competence and accessibility for underserved groups.

  • Sustainably Funded

    Government grants and donations keep all our core programs free.

Our Founder

Payal Dave

A Vision Built on Resilience

As a neurodivergent South Asian immigrant woman, Payal experienced firsthand the systemic barriers within traditional support systems. She holds multiple certifications and is a 15-time published author and 6-time National Table Tennis Champion.

Instead of accepting the fragmented resources available, she created NeuroGen to ensure that no one else has to navigate their unique brain alone.
