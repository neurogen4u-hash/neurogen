About NeuroGen Built by livedexperience. Poweredby community. NeuroGen was founded because a critical gap existed in Canada's support ecosystem — neurodivergent individuals, especially those from underserved communities, had nowhere to turn that truly understood their experience. 🧠 Community First Every program is built around peer connection and lived experience — not clinical distance. 🤝 Equity Centered We serve those most underserved — ensuring cultural competence at every level. 🌱 Sustainably Funded Government grants, charitable donations, and institutional partnerships keep all programs free. 🇨🇦 Nationally Minded Based in Toronto, serving neurodivergent Canadians from coast to coast. Why NeuroGen Exists Have you ever felt like this? If any of these sound familiar — you have found your community. 😮‍💨 "Why can't I just focus?" Your brain works differently — not deficiently. ADHD is not a flaw. It is a different operating system that the world has not yet learned to appreciate. ADHD 🌊 "I feel everything so intensely." Emotional intensity is not weakness. It is how your nervous system experiences the world — fully, deeply, and authentically. Sensory Processing 🔇 "Social situations exhaust me." Navigating a world built for neurotypical social norms is genuinely hard work. Your experience is valid. You are not alone in it. Autism Spectrum 🧩 "I don't fit anywhere." You have not found your people yet. They exist. They are here. NeuroGen was built for exactly this feeling — the one before belonging. Belonging ⚡ "So many ideas. Can't finish any." That is not failure. That is a neurodivergent brain doing what it does naturally. We help you build the scaffolding around that energy. Executive Function 💜 "Nobody understands me." The people who understand you are neurodivergent too. NeuroGen connects you with a community that gets it — because they live it. Payal Dave Founder & Executive Director 🎓 3 Graduate Degrees · 2 Countries 📜 PMP · ACSM Certified 🧠 Neurodivergent · Lived Experience 🇨🇦 Canadian Citizen · South Asian 📚 Author · 15 Published Books Our Founder Built by someonewho needed it. "The community I needed did not exist. So I built it." Payal Dave is a South Asian immigrant woman, Canadian citizen, neurodivergent entrepreneur, and author of fifteen books. She built NeuroGen because she spent years navigating the Canadian system as someone with ADHD — without culturally competent support, without a community that understood the intersection of neurodivergence and newcomer experience. Her credentials span engineering, forensic science, project management, exercise science, and entrepreneurship. Her experience spans survival, rebuilding, and the kind of resilience that only comes from having no other option. NeuroGen is not a theory. It is the organization she built because she needed it and it did not exist. Connect With Payal →
