"I am neurodivergent. I am an immigrant. I am a woman. I looked for the community that understood all of that simultaneously — and it did not exist. So I built it." Payal Dave is a South Asian immigrant woman, Canadian citizen, neurodivergent entrepreneur, six-time national athletic champion, and author of fifteen books. She built NeuroGen from lived experience — having navigated the Canadian system as someone with ADHD, without culturally competent support, without a community that understood the intersection of neurodivergence and newcomer experience. Her credentials span engineering, forensic science, project management, exercise science, and entrepreneurship. Her experience spans survival, rebuilding, and the kind of resilience that only comes from having no other option. NeuroGen is not a theory. It is the organization she built because she needed it and it did not exist. Connect With Payal →
