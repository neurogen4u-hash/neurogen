Payal Dave Founder & Executive Director 🎓 3 Degrees · PMP · ACSM Certified 🧠 Neurodivergent · Lived Experience 🇨🇦 Canadian · South Asian · Immigrant Our Founder Built by someonewho needed it. "The community I needed did not exist. So I built it." Payal Dave is a South Asian immigrant woman, Canadian citizen, neurodivergent entrepreneur, and author of fifteen books. She built NeuroGen because she spent years navigating the Canadian system as someone with ADHD — without culturally competent support, without a community that understood her experience. NeuroGen is not a theory. It is the organization she built because she needed it and it did not exist. Connect With Payal →
